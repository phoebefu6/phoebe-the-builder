  Barnard beats Fisher on 7 of 7 designs, by up to 0.1085 of power, while holding size on every design in section 2.
  Yates' size equals Fisher's EXACTLY on 5 of 9 designs: the correction
  does not repair the z-test, it turns it into an approximation of Fisher - conservatism included.
  The z-test's power lead is NOT a finding wherever section 2 flags it INFLATED - an
  inflated test detects more because it rejects more of everything.
