0.  THE CORPUS AND THE MODEL  -  synthetic, declared
====================================================================================================
  hot     10 docs  edited every    14 d  fact-bearing 60%  query share 50%   lambda 0.07143/d  mu 0.04286/d   (pricing, limits, policy)
  warm    40 docs  edited every    45 d  fact-bearing 50%  query share 35%   lambda 0.02222/d  mu 0.01111/d   (how-to guides, release notes)
  cold   150 docs  edited every   365 d  fact-bearing 30%  query share 15%   lambda 0.00274/d  mu 0.00082/d   (archive, legal, reference)
  stale(mu, T) = 1 - (1 - exp(-mu T)) / (mu T): a doc reindexed every T days, served at a uniform phase
  hash_freshness: doc-weighted, any edit.  fact_freshness: doc-weighted, fact edits.  answer_staleness: query-weighted
