
====================================================================================================
0.  THE MODEL  -  synthetic, declared
====================================================================================================
  benign      80% of traffic   legitimate   (plain requests)
  sensitive   15% of traffic   legitimate   (legitimate but sensitive: medical, legal, security)
  harmful      5% of traffic   to be refused   (requests the policy says to refuse)
  refusal_rate = sum share_c * refuse_c;  over-refusal = P(refuse | legitimate);  under-refusal = P(comply | harmful)
  measured = sum share_c * (refuse_c * recall(detector, version) + (1 - refuse_c) * fpr);  recall = phrasing mass matched
  v1 baseline            refuse per class benign 0.006, sensitive 0.120, harmful 0.900   phrasing: 'I cannot' 55%, 'I can't' 30%, 'I'm not able to' 10%
  v2 upgraded model      refuse per class benign 0.012, sensitive 0.240, harmful 0.970   phrasing: 'I cannot' 12%, 'I'm not able to' 45%, 'I won't be able to' 20%, 'I'm unable to' 10%
  v3 prompt tuned down   refuse per class benign 0.003, sensitive 0.060, harmful 0.700   phrasing: 'I cannot' 55%, 'I can't' 30%, 'I'm not able to' 10%
  detector keyword list (written on v1)   matches ["I can't", 'I cannot']   fpr 0.004
  detector keyword list, extended         matches ["I can't", 'I cannot', 'I must decline', "I won't be able to", "I'm not able to", "I'm unable to"]   fpr 0.009
  detector LLM judge                      recall 0.93   fpr 0.01

3.  THE MIX MOVES, THE MODEL DOES NOT  -  a campaign week
====================================================================================================
  harmful share 5% -> 12%: true refusal rate 6.78% -> 12.92% (measured 6.14% -> 11.33%)
  over-refusal 2.40% -> 2.41%; under-refusal 10% -> 10%: nothing about the model moved, and the refusal rate nearly doubled

====================================================================================================
4.  A FLAT AGGREGATE IS NEVER NEUTRAL  -  the twin that doubles over-refusal at the same rate
====================================================================================================
  legitimate refusal rates x2 (0.012, 0.24); aggregate held at 6.78% forces harmful refusals 90% -> 44.4%
  over-refusal 2.40% -> 4.80%, under-refusal 10% -> 56%, legitimate share of refusals 34% -> 67%
  at a fixed mix the aggregate is a weighted sum, so a rise in one error at a flat rate IS a fall in refusals of the other class

  detect the upgrade's over-refusal doubling (2.40% -> 4.80%), one-sided alpha 0.05, exact binomial
   labels    traffic sample: power   size    refusal sample: power   size
       50                    0.198  0.028                    0.795  0.027
      100                    0.305  0.027                    0.980  0.035
      300                    0.718  0.044                    1.000  0.042
     1000                    0.987  0.038                    1.000  0.044
     3000                    1.000  0.045                    1.000  0.047
  the refusal sample tests 37.6% -> 56.8% (legitimate share of detected refusals); the traffic sample tests 2.40% -> 4.80% on ~95% of its labels
  the refusal sample's own bias: legitimate share is 33.6% of TRUE refusals and 37.6% of DETECTED ones on v1 (56.8% vs 48.5% on v2) - the detector's false positives are compliant legitimate responses, so the labeller must also mark 'was this a refusal'

7.  THE DETECTOR THAT GETS THE SIGN RIGHT  -  and what it costs
====================================================================================================
  keyword list, extended       v1 measured 7.62% (true 6.78%)  v2 10.23% (true 9.41%)  v3 5.50% (true 4.64%)
  LLM judge                    v1 measured 7.24% (true 6.78%)  v2 9.66% (true 9.41%)  v3 5.27% (true 4.64%)
  share of DETECTED refusals that are false positives on v1: keyword list (written on v1) 6.1%, keyword list, extended 11.0%, LLM judge 12.9%
  a version-independent recall gets every sign right; its false positives are the price, and they land on legitimate traffic
